# Narrative Detection

## Status

Planned. Argus does not currently create narrative identities or claim that
sources participate in coordinated narratives.

## Intended boundary

A narrative is a recurring, evolving pattern of attributed claims, selected
facts, causal explanations, frames, and omissions. Similar wording alone is
not enough to establish one.

Future narrative analysis must preserve:

- the exact claims and document spans involved;
- event and entity scope;
- source, language, audience, and publication time;
- changes between document versions;
- measurable similarity and divergence signals;
- uncertainty and competing narrative groupings.

Coordination, intent, influence, and beneficiary hypotheses are separate
reasoning-layer questions. They require additional evidence and must not be
inferred from narrative similarity alone.
